5. Sharing and referral links

When you tap a share button, the Site opens the app you chose with a ready message containing your referral link. We do not read your contacts or your messages. The Site can only tell that you opened a share app and returned; it cannot see whether or to whom a message was sent.

6. Children

The Site is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has used the Site, contact us and we will help remove any data we hold.

7. Data retention and security

Data stored in your browser stays there until you clear it. Server logs kept by our hosting provider are retained only as long as needed for security and operations. We use reasonable measures to protect the Site, but no online service is completely secure.
