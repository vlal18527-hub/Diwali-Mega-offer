Spin & Voucher Rules

Last updated:

This page explains, in plain words, exactly how the game works. It is part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. The spin wheel

  • Enter your name and @username, then tap Spin.
  • Your first spin always shows "Better Luck Next Time". The brand and voucher names painted on the wheel and banner are decoration; the wheel does not award them.
  • Playing is free. No payment, deposit or purchase is needed.

2. The 10-share referral reward

  1. Tap WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram or Share to Friend.
  2. Send your link in that app, then come back to this page.
  3. A share is counted only after you opened a share app and returned (or finished your phone's share sheet). Copying the link alone, or tapping and returning instantly, is not counted. There is a short wait of 8 seconds between counted shares.
  4. When your count reaches 10/10, you receive one voucher, the Result Date opens, and you unlock one extra spin.
  5. After you use the extra spin, your count goes back to 0. Another 10 new shares earns the next voucher.
Please note: the Site can see that you opened a share app and came back, but it cannot see inside WhatsApp or Instagram. Shares are therefore counted on this basis, and we may review and cancel rewards if misuse is found.

4. How to redeem a voucher

  1. Open My Vouchers and tap Copy next to your code.
  2. Follow the redemption instructions written on the voucher card (where to use it and any conditions).
  3. Use it before its Valid Till date. Each code works once.
  4. If a code does not work, contact us with your username, voucher number and a screenshot.

Vouchers marked DEMO are test samples only and have no value.

5. Fair play

Fake shares, bots, multiple accounts or editing browser data are not allowed. We may cancel vouchers or block users who misuse the Site.

6. No affiliation

We are not affiliated with Amazon, Flipkart or any other brand shown. A voucher can be used only as its issuer describes.

7. Changes

We may change these rules, the number of vouchers or the validity period at any time. The latest version is always on this page.
